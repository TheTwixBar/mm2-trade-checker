            A quick guide to reading results and using each feature.

            trade verdicts
            WIN
            Their AI score beats yours past the fair zone. You come out ahead on value, stability, or rarity.
            FAIR
            Both sides are within the fair zone threshold. Neither player is getting significantly more.
            LOSE
            Your AI score beats theirs past the fair zone. You are giving up more than you are getting.
            confidence badges

            Every WIN or LOSE result comes with a badge showing how decisive it is. A barely WIN is much closer to fair than a big WIN.

            badge what it means what to do
            barely Just past the fair zone. The margin is small. Think twice before pushing it. The other player might not accept, or conditions could flip.
            clearly Comfortably past the threshold. A real difference. Good trade. Worth doing if you want the item.
            easily Well past the threshold. The gap is large. Strong result. You are getting meaningfully more than you are giving.
            big Way past the threshold. A dominant difference. The other player may not accept. Consider offering slightly more if you really want the deal.
            inventory tab
            1
            Add your items
            Type any item name and select it from the dropdown. Click the x(number) badge on a tag to set how many of that item you own. Hit save inventory when done.
            2
            Back up with the import string
            After saving, copy the backup string and keep it somewhere safe. If you ever lose your inventory (new device, cleared browser data), paste it back into the import box and hit import.
            3
            Use the offer generator
            Enter the item someone else has. The AI searches your inventory for the best combo to offer them, keeping a slight advantage for you. Use the favor slider to control how aggressive the margin is: "fair" aims for even, "max" tries to build in the largest possible gain.
            4
            Read the three options
            The offer generator shows up to three different combos ranked best to worst. Option 1 is always the closest to your target gain. Options 2 and 3 are alternatives in case option 1 uses an item you do not want to give away.
            trade tab
            1
            Enter both sides
            Add your items on the left, their items on the right. Use the x(number) badge if either side has multiple of the same item.
            2
            Read the result
            The verdict tells you WIN, LOSE, or FAIR. The confidence badge tells you by how much. Check the stability section for warnings on items that are dropping or hard to retrade.

            This trade checker does not just compare raw mm2 values. It factors in stability, demand, rarity, liquidity, and more to produce an AI score that reflects what an item is actually worth to trade with right now.

            step 1: base value
            01
            Use range midpoint when available
            If an item has a value range (e.g. 200 to 400), the midpoint (300) is used as its base, not the listed value. If no range exists, the listed mm2 value is used directly.
            step 2: ai score (per item)
            02
            Demand bonus (log-scaled)
            Higher demand adds a bonus to the score, but using a curve rather than a flat rate. The jump from demand 2 to 4 matters more than the jump from 8 to 10, which is more realistic. An item with demand 5 gets roughly +18%.
            03
            Rarity bonus (log-scaled)
            Same curve as demand. Rarity above average adds a bonus that grows fast at low values and slows at high values. An item with rarity 4 gets roughly +10%.
            04
            Demand and rarity interaction
            Items that are both rare and in high demand get an extra bonus on top of the individual bonuses. An item that is hard to get AND everyone wants is worth more than just the sum of its demand plus rarity scores.
            05
            Stability bonus or penalty
            Each stability status has a multiplier. Items trending up are worth more to hold and easier to retrade. Items trending down are worth less. The delta from Stable is weighted at x0.75 and added to the score.
            formula
            bonus = demand_bonus + rarity_bonus + interaction + stab_bonus - liquidity_penalty
            multiplier = 1.0 + bonus clamped to 0.60 to 1.70
            ai_score = base_value x multiplier
            06
            Liquidity penalty
            Low-value items are harder to trade away on their own, so they receive a penalty to reflect that. Items worth 1000+ have no penalty. Items worth under 50 carry up to a -18% penalty.
            tiers
            1000+ value: no penalty
            200 to 999: -4%
            50 to 199: -10%
            under 50: -18%
            stability multipliers
            status multiplier effect on score
            Rising x1.90 trending up strongly
            Hyped x1.60 high community interest
            Doing Well x1.40 moderate upward trend
            Overpaid For x1.25 people pay above value, good to hold
            Stabilizing x1.08 settling after movement
            Recovering x1.07 bouncing back slightly
            Stable x1.00no change, this is the baseline
            N/A x0.90unknown status, slight caution
            Fluctuating x0.82unstable price, usually new items that will drop
            Losing Hype x0.68demand is fading
            Underpaid For x0.55people trade below value, bad to receive
            Decreasing x0.50actively losing value
            step 3: bundle penalty (your side only)
            07
            Multi-item trades are worth less
            When you give more than one item, a penalty is applied per extra item. A single high-value item is easier to retrade than three mid-tier items worth the same total. The penalty also scales up if your items are low-value, since those are the hardest to move individually. This penalty only applies to your side, not theirs.
            example
            you give 3 items: base penalty = 2 x 2.5% = 5%
            if your items average low-value: penalty scales up further
            max penalty capped at 22%
            step 4: result
            08
            Dynamic fair zone threshold
            The fair zone scales with trade size. The threshold is 4.5% of the total trade value, with a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 400. Larger trades get a wider margin before tipping into win or lose. The cap at 400 prevents very high-value trades from having a blind spot that is too large.
            example
            trade total = 1000 then threshold = max(3, min(45, 400)) = 45
            if |their_ai minus your_ai| is 45 or less then fair
            09
            Stability tiebreaker
            If a trade lands in the fair zone on value, stability is checked. If their average stability multiplier is more than 0.08 below yours, it flips to lose. If theirs is more than 0.08 above yours, it changes to win.
            10
            Rarity tiebreaker
            If the trade is still fair after the stability check, rarity is compared. If your highest rarity item is 1.5 or more points rarer than theirs, the result changes to lose since you are giving up something harder to get back.
            The stability tiebreaker and rarity tiebreaker only apply when the trade is already inside the fair zone. They are not overrides.
