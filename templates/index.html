            This trade checker does NOT just compare raw mm2 values, it also factors in stability, demand, rarity, and even more. You can see how it calculates the "ai" score below...

            step 1: base value
            01
            Use range midpoint when available
            If an item has a value range (e.g. 200–400), the midpoint (300) is used as its base, not the listed value. If no range exists, the listed mm2 value is used directly.
            step 2: ai score (per item)
            02
            Demand bonus
            Demand above 2 adds a bonus. Each point above 2 adds +6% to the multiplier. An item with demand 5 adds +18% (3 points × 6%).
            03
            Rarity bonus
            Rarity above 1.5 adds a bonus. Each point above 1.5 adds +4%. An item with rarity 4 adds +10% (2.5 points × 4%).
            04
            Stability bonus or penalty
            Each stability status has a multiplier. The delta from 1.0 (Stable) is weighted at ×0.75 and added to the score. A "rising" item (×1.90) adds +67.5%. A "decreasing" item (×0.50) subtracts 37.5%.
            formula
            bonus = demand_bonus + rarity_bonus + stab_bonus
            multiplier = 1.0 + bonus ← clamped to 0.70–1.60
            ai_score = base_value × multiplier
            stability multipliers
            status multiplier effect on score
            Rising ×1.90 trending
            Hyped ×1.60 high interest
            Doing Well ×1.40 moderate interest
            Overpaid For ×1.25 moderate interest
            Stabilizing ×1.08 not much of change
            Recovering ×1.07 not much of change
            Stable ×1.00no change. this is the baseline
            Fluctuating ×0.82penalty as this tag is usually given to newly released items, which usually will drop in value
            Losing Hype ×0.68demand fading
            Underpaid For ×0.55penalty. people will often trade below value
            Decreasing ×0.50very large penalty. nobody likes this item
            step 3: bundle penalty (your side only)
            05
            Multi-item trades are worth less
            When you give more than one item, a 3% penalty is applied per extra item (capped at −20%). This is liquidity; a single high-value item is easier to retrade than three mid-tier items worth the same total. This penalty only applies to your side, not theirs.
            example
            you give 3 items → penalty = 1.0 − (2 × 0.03) = ×0.94
            your ai total × 0.94 ← your side worth 6% less
            step 4: result
            06
            Dynamic fair zone threshold
            The "fair zone" isn't a fixed number; it will scale with the trade size. The threshold is 4.5% of the total trade value, with a minimum floor of 3. Larger trades have a larger margin before they go into win or lose.
            example
            trade total = 1000 → threshold = max(3, 45) = ±45
            if |their_ai − your_ai| ≤ 45 → fair
            07
            Stability tiebreaker
            If a trade lands in the fair zone on value, stability is checked. If their average stability multiplier is more than 0.08 below yours, it flips to lose. If theirs is more than 0.08 above yours, it changes to win.
            08
            Rarity tiebreaker
            If the trade is still fair after the stability check, rarity is compared. If your highest rarity item is 1.5+ points rarer than theirs, the result changes to lose as you're giving up something harder to get back.
            The stability tiebreaker and rarity tiebreaker only apply when the trade is already inside the fair zone. They are not overrides.
            what the verdicts mean
            WIN
            Their AI score exceeds yours beyond the fair zone. You come out ahead on value, stability, or rarity.
            FAIR
            Both sides are within the dynamic threshold. Neither player is getting significantly more.
            LOSE
            Your AI score exceeds theirs beyond the fair zone. You're giving up more than you're getting.
